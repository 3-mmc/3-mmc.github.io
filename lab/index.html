Three tin-oxide gas sensors read through an ADS1115, with an SHT41 for temperature and humidity, sampled every 10 seconds by an ESP32-C3. The board is on a home network and has no public address, so this page is a mirror: a relay copies its readings out on an interval, and the timestamp above says how old that copy is. The board's own page updates live.

Values are millivolts at the sensor, and Rs/R0 against a clean-air baseline where one has been captured. Deliberately not ppm: these are broadband elements with no selectivity, and the ppm formulas quoted for them are curve fits to a single-gas calibration chart. The sensors are currently burning in, so the baselines are not yet trustworthy.
