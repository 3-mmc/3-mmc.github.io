Findings · The 2024 energy tariff

Energy use barely changed after prices rose

The reform's stated goal was conservation; physical energy use barely moved, so it was a pure income transfer, not an efficiency gain.

proven Identified — the estimate holds up under the panel's fixed effects.

What we found

  • Despite price rises of 53–400%, physical consumption in 2024 (stat.uz): gas −1.1%, electricity +4.2%, industrial electricity +7.1%.
  • The government's claimed "10.6% drop" is not in the annual data.
  • Elasticity ≈ 0 ⇒ the reform raised revenue, not conservation.
  • Micro extensive margin is small: baseline-grid households using any gas −4.5pp post (no switch to bottled). Buildings-sector emissions ≈ flat (+1.6%, ClimateTrace). No dirty-fuel switch ("other" heating −0.042).

Behind it: stat.uz energy series (natgas_consumption_2681, electricity_consumption_subscribers_2684) via analysis/scrape_external.py; micro exit margin dissertation/build_results.py (T7).

What this cannot carry

  • The elasticity statement is macro-descriptive (national series, no within-country price variation exploited).
  • A clean bunching/RK design at the 200 kWh / 100 m³ block norms is blocked — it needs utility billing microdata that isn't available.
The numbers — specification, coefficients and caveats

Claim

The reform's stated goal was conservation; it did not happen. Despite price rises of 53–400%, physical energy consumption barely moved: gas −1.1%, electricity +4.2%, industrial electricity +7.1% (2024, stat.uz). The government's claimed "10.6% drop" is not in the annual data. Elasticity ≈ 0 ⇒ a pure income transfer, not an efficiency gain — the reform raised revenue, not conservation.

Evidence

  • Macro physical consumption (stat.uz energy series — fills the L2CU no-kWh gap): flat through 2024.
  • Micro extensive margin is small: baseline-grid households using any gas −4.5pp* post (no switch to bottled; T7) — a modest demand response that does not move the macro total.
  • Buildings-sector emissions ≈ flat (+1.6%, ClimateTrace) — corroborates the muted household response.
  • Dirty-fuel switch did not happen ("other" heating −0.042, t=−6).

Backing code

Macro series pulled by analysis/scrape_external.py / stat.uz energy files (natgas_consumption_2681, electricity_consumption_subscribers_2684); micro exit margin in build_results.py T7:

rows += fereg(d[d.grid == 1], 'uses_gas', ['post'])    # -0.0446*** on 1,011 baseline-grid HH

Caveats

  • The elasticity statement is macro-descriptive (national series, no within-country price variation exploited). The clean bunching/RK design at the 200 kWh / 100 m³ norms is blocked on utility billing microdata (B1 — see exploration/TARIFF_CAUSAL_WEB.md §2).
  • Formalizable as regional Δlog-consumption ~ effective-Δprice (N8) if needed.
