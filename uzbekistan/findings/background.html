descriptive Real in the data, but not causally identified.

A reservoir of facts from the wide all-data exploration. None of these is an identified causal result — they're context, and several could seed an entirely different project.

  • Regional inequality is widening. No β-convergence; σ-divergence (cross-region SD of log real income 0.28→0.35, 2018–25). Tashkent city is 3.6× Karakalpakstan. (reg_convergence.py, subnational_growth*.py)
  • 2017 liberalization re-wired the economy. Currency unification (~170% devaluation), credit 10→30% of GDP, trade 39→62% of GDP, internet 32→77%. (wdi_break.py)
  • Migration. 13–18% of households have a member abroad; Russia is ~80% of destinations and barely moved after Feb 2022; remittances are 14% of GDP (3rd most dependent globally). (l2cu_migration.py)
  • Attitudes. "Government fights corruption well" fell 93%→70% (2019–25) — the sharpest erosion — while WGI governance indicators improved on every dimension: an expectations-outrun-delivery paradox. (l2cu_views.py, fetch_wgi.py)
  • Female higher-ed revolution. Enrolment F:M ratio 0.70 (2011) → 1.18 (2024), but attainment is still male-skewed and female youth unemployment ratio doubled. (mics_edu.py)
  • Network infrastructure is urban-locked regardless of wealth (MICS6). Piped water 1.5% (poorest) → 70% (richest); flush-to-sewer 0% → 81%; urban coefficients survive wealth controls — rhymes with the grid-incidence result. (mics_hh*.py)
  • Irrigation, not rain. NDVI predicts grain output (β=1.43), rainfall doesn't. (district_climate.py)
