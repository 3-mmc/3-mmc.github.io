Findings · The 2024 energy tariff

Compensation for the tariff reached 16 households

The income-targeted compensation for the tariff failed structurally — it reached almost no one — because the losers are defined by grid access, not income.

proven Identified — the estimate holds up under the panel's fixed effects.

What we found

  • The targeted utility-compensation transfer (benefit_17) appears for ~16 households in the entire L2CU panel (~2,500 HH).
  • Pension/benefit recipients grew only +2.4% in 2024 — the safety net did not expand — against a bill shock hitting ~54% of households (the grid share).
  • The effective transfer instead ran through untargeted agricultural subsidies.
  • Because losers are defined by grid access, not income (a Hanna–Olken targeting mismatch), income-targeting was structurally incapable of finding them. The implied fix is exposure-indexed (grid × winter) compensation.

Behind it: analysis/02_coding_and_availability.py. Context: iCRAFT tranche ($7.5M, Jun 2024) targeted "poorest grid households" — an under-used direct channel. Oct 2025: compensation transfer quadrupled (270k → 1M sum) — a built-in second event not yet exploited.

What this cannot carry

  • Take-up of ~16 HH also blocks an RD design on compensation eligibility — that would need administrative rolls.
The numbers — specification, coefficients and caveats

Claim

The income-targeted compensation channel failed structurally: the targeted utility-compensation transfer (benefit_17) appears for ~16 households in the entire L2CU panel, and pension/benefit recipients grew only +2.4% in 2024 — the safety net did not expand. The effective transfer ran through untargeted agricultural subsidies instead. Because the losers are defined by grid access, not income (Hanna–Olken mismatch), income-targeting was structurally incapable of finding them; the policy implication is exposure-indexed (grid × winter) compensation.

Evidence

  • benefit_17 (utility compensation): ~16 HH ever, in a panel of ~2,500.
  • Safety-net caseload +2.4% (2024) vs an energy bill shock hitting ~54% of households (grid share).
  • iCRAFT tranche ($7.5M, Jun 2024) targeted "poorest grid households" — the under-used direct channel.
  • Oct 2025: compensation transfer quadrupled (270k→1M sum) — a built-in second event (N4) for testing reversal, not yet exploited.

Caveats

  • Take-up ≈16 HH also blocks the RD design on compensation eligibility (B2) — needs admin rolls.
