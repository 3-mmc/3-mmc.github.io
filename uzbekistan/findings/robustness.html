Claims that did not survive further testing

The honesty ledger — things that looked true in a naive comparison but did not survive household fixed effects, and the claims we therefore dropped.

revised The honesty ledger — what did not survive a harder test.

Three revisions

  1. Dose-response is NULL under household FE — monotonicity claim WITHDRAWN. post × baseline-bill quartile on utility distress: Q4−Q1 ≈ +0.002 (t=0.2). Bills scaled with baseline use (post×ln-base on ln-payment +0.072) but distress did not — plausibly because big users are richer. The incidence margin is winter × grid, not bill volume.
  2. Grid incomes did NOT rise differentially — "urban income boom" SOFTENED. post×grid on ln(HH cash income) = −0.01 (t=−0.1). Masking worked through a broad-based nominal income boom (~+20%/yr both groups) plus seasonal averaging, not a differential urban boom. Side benefit: no differential-income confound on the winter-bite estimates.
  3. Exit margin exists but is small. Baseline-grid HH using any gas −4.5pp post; no switch to bottled. Sits alongside elasticity ≈ 0.

Standing "don't claim" list

  • ❌ "The tariff bought reliability" → "imports restored it, the tariff repriced it."
  • ❌ Orchestrated balancing act (no decree/statement evidence of intent).
  • ❌ Industrial policy as achieved productive reallocation (unfalsifiable in-window).
  • ❌ Reform impoverished urban households (bill shock masked by income gains ≠ immiseration).
  • ❌ Political-legitimacy cost (right-track −0.013 does not survive the event study — unconfirmed).

The numbers — specification, coefficients and caveats

Every results paragraph must respect these revisions (2026-06-11). They are strengths — show them.

1. Dose-response is NULL under household FE — monotonicity claim WITHDRAWN

post × baseline-bill quartile on utility distress: Q4−Q1 ≈ +0.002 (t=0.2). The earlier "distress rises monotonically with pre-use" was a naive group comparison. Bills scaled with baseline use (post×ln-base on ln-payment +0.072**) but distress did not — plausibly because big users are richer. The incidence margin is winter × grid, not bill volume.

2. Grid incomes did NOT rise differentially — "urban income boom" claim SOFTENED

post×grid on ln(HH cash income) = −0.01 (t=−0.1). Masking worked through a broad-based nominal income boom (~+20%/yr both groups) plus seasonal averaging — not a differential urban boom. Side benefit: no differential-income confound on the winter-bite estimates.

3. Exit margin exists but is small

Baseline-grid HH using any gas −4.5pp*** post; no switch to bottled (piped share among users +1.4pp). Note alongside elasticity ≈ 0.

Standing don't-claim list (from the claim table)

  • ❌ "The tariff bought reliability" → "imports restored it, the tariff repriced it"
  • ❌ Orchestrated balancing act (no decree/statement evidence of intent)
  • ❌ Industrial policy as achieved productive reallocation (unfalsifiable in-window)
  • ❌ Reform impoverished urban households (bill shock masked by income gains ≠ immiseration)
  • ❌ Political-legitimacy cost (right-track −0.013 does not survive the event study — unconfirmed; first cut-candidate)
