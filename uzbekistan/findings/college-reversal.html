Banked — the compulsory-college reversal cohort cliff

Uzbekistan's 1997 compulsory-vocational reform and its 2017 reversal produce one of the sharpest cohort discontinuities in survey data — possibly the only modern case of a state removing compulsory schooling years — but the outcome window hasn't matured.

banked Real, but the outcome window has not matured.

The find

  • % with secondary-specialized vocational education (SSVE) by birth cohort: 58→76 (forcing-in, 1990→93), plateaus at 77–80 (1994–99), then collapses 72 → 56 → 23 → 7% across cohorts 2000→2003 (release).
  • The reversal direction does not exist in the compulsory-schooling literature.

Why it's banked, not used

  • The sensational downstream result (released-cohort women: married-by-20 7%→24.7%) is a period effect — the 2021–22 post-COVID marriage surge. An age-contrast DiD kills it (hazards tripled at all ages; DiD t=−0.5).
  • University attendance at the release margin is confounded by the simultaneous 2018–24 tertiary expansion.
  • L2CU's roster education variable contradicts MICS6 (55% vs ~7–16% SSVE for born 2005) — miscoded, unusable without a codebook deep-dive.
  • Needs MICS7 / LFS microdata circa 2026+, when released cohorts hit their mid-20s outside the COVID surge.

What's writable now: a first-stage + detracking-incidence note — who left the colleges, by region/wealth/sex (MICS6 has all three).

Behind it: exploration/NE_HUNT_2026-06.md; MICS6 (hl.sav, wm.sav).
