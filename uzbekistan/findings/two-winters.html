Findings · The 2024 energy tariff

Payment problems spiked twice, for two different reasons

Grid-household payment distress spikes exactly twice — the 2022/23 supply crisis and the 2024/25 price winter — with a clean zero in between.

proven Identified — the estimate holds up under the panel's fixed effects.

What we found

  • 2022/23 crisis winter: grid × Feb–Apr 23 = +0.026 (gas physically ran out).
  • 2024/25 price winter: grid × Nov 24–Jan 25 = +0.030 (the bill shock).
  • The pre-reform reference quarter is clean at zero.
  • Same households, same season, two allocation mechanisms — rationing by outage became rationing by bill.

The numbers — specification, coefficients and caveats

Claim

Grid-household payment distress spikes exactly twice in the panel: the 2022/23 supply-crisis winter (grid×Feb–Apr 23 = +0.026***, when gas physically ran out) and the 2024/25 price winter (grid×Nov 24–Jan 25 = +0.030***), with the pre-reform reference quarter clean at zero. Same households, same season, two allocation mechanisms: rationing by outage became rationing by bill. The "pre-trend violations" earlier event studies worried about are the 2022/23 crisis — a feature, not a bug, once named.

Backing code

dissertation/build_results.py T6 (HH FE + round FE, ref = Feb–Apr 2024):

ev = d[(d['round'] >= 49) & (d['round'] <= 82)].dropna(subset=['util_distress']).copy()
ev['evq'] = ((ev['round'] - 69) // 3).astype(int)     # 3-round (quarterly) bins; round 69 = May 2024
Xc = []
for q in sorted(ev.evq.unique()):
    if q == -1:                                       # ref = quarter before reform (Feb-Apr 2024)
        continue
    ev[f'g_eq{q}'] = ev.grid * (ev.evq == q)
    Xc.append(f'g_eq{q}')
rows = fereg(ev, 'util_distress', Xc, timefe='round')  # round FE absorbs common time

Caveats

  • Method warning (from the political-legitimacy dead end): crude half-year bins blur the winter spike and over-reject — quarterly bins + round FE (or calendar-month FE) are mandatory for any seasonal outcome. See exploration/TARIFF_CAUSAL_WEB.md §4.
