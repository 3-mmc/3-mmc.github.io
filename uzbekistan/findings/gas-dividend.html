The end of the gas dividend

The rent that paid for cheap gas evaporated exactly as households were asked to pay more — this is the "why now."

descriptive Real in the data, but not causally identified.

What we found

  • Gas production collapsed −37% (65,958 mln m³ in 2010 → 41,524 in 2024), consumption ~flat (~47,000).
  • The net balance flipped from +13,164 (2010 export surplus) to net importer in 2023 (−850), widening to −5,534 in 2024 (the reform year) — while Uzbekistan still contractually exported to China (BRI Line C).
  • Gas rents fell from 19.8% of GDP (2008) to single digits; fuel exports from 24.9% of merchandise exports (2018) to ~5%.
  • Russian gas imports began Oct 2023 → reliability recovery winter 2023/24.

The 2022/23 outage winter and the 2024 tariff are two faces of the same resource collapse.

Behind it: exploration/outputs/uzb_gas_balance.csv (stat.uz national gas balance); exploration/profile_statuz.py. CADGAT/BP series to 2020 shows the same trajectory at slightly different levels.

What this cannot carry

  • One country, one time series — motivation, not a causal estimate. The gas-flip → fiscal-pressure → reform chain is a narrative, not an identified claim.
