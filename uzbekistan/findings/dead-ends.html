Approaches that were tried and abandoned

Two systematic hunts for an alternative identification strategy.

Two systematic hunts for an alternative identification strategy. Recorded so they don't get re-hunted — each was killed honestly, usually by running the first stage / sharpness test first.

  • Syrdarya mandatory-health-insurance pilot (Jul 2021), regional DiD. Recorded health-services volume +0.186 (rank 1/14 placebos) — but that's a billing series (MHI moves out-of-pocket activity onto the books). Real utilization (treated patients): +0.003, rank 14/14. A formalization-of-finance story, not a welfare story. Could revive with regional out-of-pocket/HBS data; the staggered rollout is the country's cleanest design if utilization outcomes are obtained.
  • 2022 ruble windfall to migrant households (L2CU shift-share) — KILLED. First stage fails: among migrant households, remittance receipt is flat (~45%), median amount flat ($300), straight through 2022. The macro remittance boom (+50% in BoP data) is invisible in the micro module.
  • Pension-eligibility RD at 55 (women) / 60 (men) — KILLED. P(receives pension) climbs smoothly through the eligibility age; early-retirement, disability and survivor pensions destroy the discontinuity. No sharpness.
  • 2022/23 energy crisis → respiratory illness by gas access — KILLED. Regional flu-incidence series is unusable (within-region swings of two orders of magnitude — reporting artifacts). Wrong-signed and insignificant anyway.
  • Compulsory preschool year (2021/22) birth-month RD (MICS6) — KILLED. ECE attendance is ~95–99% for all cohorts 2015–2017 (near-universal by survey year). No exploitable eligibility discontinuity.

Bottom line: across regional reforms, single-region pilots, cohort discontinuities, eligibility RDs, external shocks, and historical persistence, the tariff reform was the only design in hand with a first stage, mature outcomes, and credible identification simultaneously. The one asset to bank is the compulsory-college reversal (see note 17).
