Findings · The tariff

The frozen bill — a winter-shaped price shock

The 1 May 2024 tariff raised every household's energy bill ~70%, but the extra bite lands almost entirely in winter, on grid (piped-gas) households.

proven Identified — the estimate holds up under the panel's fixed effects.

What we found

  • The subsidy era shows up as a frozen nominal price: median electricity payment sat at exactly 50,000 UZS from 2021 through 2023 (so the real price fell ~10%/yr), then jumped +60% in 2024 and +25% in 2025.
  • After the reform, post = +0.53 log points on total energy payment (t=28) — a ~70% rise for everyone.
  • The grid premium exists only in winter: post × grid × winter = +0.17 (t=5.3). Grid households' heating-season bills roughly doubled (piped-gas median Dec 2023 100k → Dec 2024 200k UZS).
  • First stage scales with baseline use: post × ln(baseline bill) on ln(payment) = +0.072 — the block-tariff structure binding.

Behind it: L2CU household panel; canonical run dissertation/build_results.py (T2), first cut exploration/claude_newangles.py. Exposure = modal pre-reform gas type (rounds ≤68); canonical sample 1,862 households.

What this cannot carry

  • Payments are expenditures (price × quantity, possibly lagged ~1 month, heaped at round numbers) — there's no kWh denominator in the data.
  • The May→Aug 2024 ramp partly reflects payment lags and the scheduled block phase-in, not just the price change.

Backing code

Canonical run: dissertation/build_results.py (T2 block). Exposure definition and spec:

# baseline grid exposure: modal pre-reform gas type (rounds <= 68)
pre = d[d['round'] <= 68]
grid = (pre.assign(p=(pre.gas_type.astype('string').str.strip() == 'Piped/central gas').astype(float))
        .groupby('hhid')['p'].mean().round().rename('grid'))
d = d.merge(grid, on='hhid', how='left')
d = d.dropna(subset=['grid'])                      # CANONICAL SAMPLE (1,862 HH)

d['pay_total'] = d[['electricity_payment', 'gas_payment_piped', 'gas_payment_liquid']].fillna(0).sum(axis=1)
d.loc[d.electricity_payment.isna(), 'pay_total'] = np.nan
d['ln_pay_total'] = np.log1p(d['pay_total'])

# interactions
d['p_g'] = d.post * d.grid
d['p_w'] = d.post * d.winter          # winter = Dec-Mar
d['g_w'] = d.grid * d.winter
d['p_g_w'] = d.post * d.grid * d.winter

# T2 — first stage (HH FE + calendar-month FE, CR0 cluster-HH; see [[The estimator]])
rows = (fereg(d, 'ln_pay_total', ['post', 'p_g'])
        + fereg(d, 'ln_pay_total', ['post', 'p_g', 'p_w', 'g_w', 'p_g_w'])
        + fereg(d, 'ln_pay_elec', ['post'])
        + fereg(d[d.grid == 1], 'ln_pay_gas', ['post', 'p_w']))

Caveats

  • Payments are expenditures (price × quantity, possibly lagged ~1 month, heaped at round numbers); no kWh denominator (the B1 blocker — see Data caveats).
  • The May→Aug 2024 ramp partly reflects payment lags and the scheduled block phase-in.
