The spine

The strip under the masthead is not decoration.

Every wealth cell in the country, sorted by how far it sits from an 1888 railway

21,382 Meta Relative Wealth Index cells. The Trans-Caspian line was laid between 1888 and 1906 to move cotton. Cells within 20 km of it are still measurably richer — about a third of a standard deviation — and the gradient falls away monotonically for 200 km.

Why it matters here

The railway corridor runs Bukhara → Samarkand → Jizzakh → Tashkent. So does the Soviet gas trunk that followed it. So does the grid whose customers paid for the 2024 tariff.

Which is the uncomfortable part: a universal energy subsidy in a country like this is not a subsidy to the poor. It is a subsidy to whoever a 19th-century railway and a 20th-century pipeline happened to reach.
