Take the data apart yourself

Four tools over the same collection: a map, a scatter, a long regional panel and the full World Bank series for Uzbekistan. Nothing here is modelled — these are the raw series, and every chart will hand you its table.

Put any regional series on the map

Wealth and climate exist for all 199 districts; stat.uz publishes for the 14 regions only. Switching resolution shows you what each source can and cannot actually resolve.

Plot any two regional series against each other

Each point is one region in one year. The dashed line is an ordinary least-squares fit — descriptive only, with no controls and no fixed effects.
