Climate & water

Uzbekistan farms a desert. Almost all cropland is irrigated, which means the weather variable that matters is not how much rain fell — it is how green things actually got. That distinction has teeth: vegetation greenness predicts grain output here, and rainfall does not. If you are looking for a weather instrument in this country, rain is the wrong one.

Water

Uzbekistan withdraws more freshwater than its own rivers generate — the balance comes from upstream, and the Aral Sea is what the shortfall cost.
