Topic

Gold

Uzbekistan mines roughly a hundred tonnes of gold a year, and by law the central bank gets first refusal on all of it. The result is one of the most gold-concentrated reserve portfolios on earth: as the metal's price ran up after 2019, the bank's gold pile grew while its dollar buffer shrank. This page is the public, descriptive record of that — exports, reserve composition, and the world price that drives both.

Resource rents in context

Mineral rents rose as gas rents collapsed. The composition of Uzbekistan's resource income changed completely within about fifteen years.

Natural resource rents, % of GDP

World Bank WDI. This is the macro backdrop to the end of the gas dividend: one rent stream ran down while another ran up, but they do not accrue to the same people or through the same institutions.

How far these numbers go

  • This page is the descriptive public record: exports, reserve composition and the world price. It makes no claim about what any of it caused.
  • Reserve values move with revaluation as well as with purchases. A rising gold share can mean the bank bought metal, or simply that the metal it already held became worth more. Separating the two needs tonnage, not the dollar series shown here.
  • "Tracked exports" is the set of commodity lines in the Comtrade pull behind this page, not total merchandise trade — so the share is a share of that set.
