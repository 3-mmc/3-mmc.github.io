Prices & money

Uzbek headline inflation has sat near 10% for years, which sounds like stability. In 2024 it was the opposite: every comparable economy in the region disinflated sharply that year and Uzbekistan did not. Food prices fell hard enough to cancel out an energy shock in the same basket — so the aggregate stayed still while its components moved violently in opposite directions.
