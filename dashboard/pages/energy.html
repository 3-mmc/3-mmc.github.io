Electricity & gas

Uzbekistan spent thirty years as a gas exporter and then, quite suddenly, stopped being one. Production fell by a third while consumption held flat; in 2023 the country became a net importer. That is the pressure behind everything else on this page — the outage winter, the import deal, and the tariff that made households pay for the fix.
