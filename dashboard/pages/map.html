The map

Uzbekistan's regions differ in size by nearly five hundred times: Karakalpakstan covers 166,000 km², Tashkent city 351. On a true-area map the places where most people actually live are specks. So every layer here comes two ways — the real geography, and a tile cartogram that gives each region the same amount of ink.

Where people are

Population is not published per region in this collection, so density here is estimated — dwellings times mean household size, over the region's area. Treat it as an order of magnitude, not a census.
