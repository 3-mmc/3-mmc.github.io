Topic

Agriculture

Half of Uzbek households receive some agricultural income, and almost all of the country's farmland is irrigated rather than rainfed. Two things make the sector worth looking at closely: cotton is bought at a price the state sets rather than one the market clears, and the 2024 reform package moved a great deal of money into the sector at exactly the moment household energy bills rose.

Cotton against everything else

Between 2022 and 2024, 43 of 52 FAO commodities rose in price — median +55%. The two largest falls were both cotton.

The sector in the national accounts

Agriculture's share of output has fallen steadily while its share of employment has fallen faster — the standard structural-transformation pattern, with an irrigation twist.

Value added, employment and yield

Water

Nearly all agricultural land here is irrigated, and agriculture takes almost all the freshwater withdrawn.

Which is why rainfall is a weak weather control in this country: greenness predicts grain output, rain does not. See Climate & water.
