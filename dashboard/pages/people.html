People & work

Uzbekistan has around 37 million people, a young population, and one of the most remittance-dependent economies in the world — between 13% and 18% of households have a member working abroad, and about four in five of them are in Russia. This page covers what people do for work, what they are paid, who leaves, and what the health and education systems look like region by region.
