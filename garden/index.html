Calibrate the soil probe

Leave the probe where it lives, and take both ends in one watering: just before you water, save the dry point — whatever it reads then is your zero. Water thoroughly, let it drain half an hour, save the wet point.

Dry does not mean bone dry. It is the driest you intend to let the pot get, so the tree never has to go short for it. Soil dried right out stops conducting and reads the same as open air, which puts zero where the soil can never reach.

Save the air point once, with the probe lifted clear of the soil. It measures the board rather than the soil, and it is what lets a disconnected probe be told from dry soil and readings be corrected for temperature. Clearing keeps it.
